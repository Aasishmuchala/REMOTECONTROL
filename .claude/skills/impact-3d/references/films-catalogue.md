# IMPACT 3D film catalogue — what the channel's videos actually do

Source: scene-by-scene analyses of 17 films on the IMPACT 3D YouTube channel
(@manjivagjiyani, channel id UCzf5i6tiFHp2efNgt7RATjQ), run through a
server-side video analyser because the build sandbox cannot open YouTube.
Each entry lists the observed structure, camera, light, staging and sound.
The cross-film patterns at the end are what the playbooks in this skill are
built on. Video ids are given so the films can be re-watched.

## 1. Cross-film patterns

**Runtime and rhythm**
- 2018–2020 films run 2:00–3:30; 2025–2026 films run 1:30–1:40.
- Shot length is 3–5 s almost everywhere; 8–12 s only for aerial openers
  and the occasional long pan. A 2:30 film has 40–55 shots.
- Cuts are straight cuts on the music; no wipes, no speed ramps.

**The arc (every residential film)**
1. Cold open on nature or context, not the building: macro palm frond,
   dew on grass, lotus with water drops, sun through leaves, birds crossing a
   hazy sky, tree reflections in still water, or a misty sunrise aerial with
   god rays. 3–15 s.
2. Title card (white text on black, or on a light textured card).
3. Building reveal: low-angle tilt-up framed by foliage, then a wide.
4. Arrival by car: aerial follow, rear track, lateral track over a bridge,
   car turning in at the branded entrance wall, car stopping at the porch.
   The car is the viewer's vehicle. Often a black or grey luxury sedan/SUV.
5. Lobby with receptionist, then the amenity inventory: one static frame
   per amenity (lounge, library, gym, games room, kids room, banquet, pool,
   yoga, cinema, salon, sports court, playground, cafe).
6. Apartment or villa interiors: living, dining, kitchen, master bedroom,
   balcony, each with one styled close-up.
7. Dusk: pool lit, balcony with candles, interior glow through glass.
8. Night: facade with lit windows, fire pit, string lights, night aerial,
   sometimes searchlights or fireworks.
9. Credits: project logo, developer, architect, landscape architect,
   "Visual by / A Film By IMPACT 3D", RERA number and a disclaimer card.

**Camera**
- Static frames dominate older films; from 2023 on there are slow dollies,
  lateral tracks, aerial glides, top-down orbits and a "tilt up from a
  balcony grid" move. Nothing fast.
- Recurring signature frames:
  - top-down through a ring/tiered pendant onto a dining or coffee table;
  - top-down into a spiral stair or a courtyard from the tower top;
  - view through wood slats, a jaali, palm leaves or a half-open door;
  - the same frame day then dusk (match cut) as the lights come on;
  - low-angle straight up at towers with trees in the frame corners;
  - camera at water level looking across a pool to umbrellas;
  - symmetrical tower reflected in a still lake or lap pool;
  - balcony looking in through sliding glass, warm inside vs cool outside;
  - handheld-style low follow of a walking couple with sun flare through
    leaves.

**Light**
- Golden hour and blue hour are the default mood for exteriors; midday
  appears only for sports courts and playgrounds.
- Interiors are lit by daylight through sheers or louvers, with warm
  practicals (cove LED, pendants, lamps) taking over at dusk.
- Lens flare and starburst through leaves are used deliberately, several
  times per film.
- Every film ends at night with warm interior light against a deep blue sky.

**Staging**
- Interiors carry one "life" detail per frame: steaming pot, coffee with
  latte art, croissants and fruit on a tray, pancakes on the island,
  pomegranates on marble, an open book on a bench, glasses on a magazine,
  a towel embroidered with the project logo, spa bottles on a tray.
- 3D people appear from 2020 on and are everywhere by 2023: a walking
  couple, a father and child, joggers, a receptionist, players on courts,
  women meditating, kids in a ball pit. They are mid-distance, never posed
  at the camera.
- Indian cues used without irony: jhoola (wooden swing), Ganesha with lamp
  and flowers, mandir with marigold garlands, jaali and golden screens,
  brick entrance with a tiled roof, clay urns with lotus, carrom boards.
- Brand moments: the project logo on the entrance wall in raking light,
  on treadmill screens, on towels, on plates, lit at the club reception.

**Sound**
- Older films: one piano track, uplifting, sometimes with strings.
- 2023 films: acoustic guitar fingerpicking, or an Indian-fusion score
  (flute, sitar, percussion) plus a diegetic layer of birds, water, car
  engine, children, tennis hits, crickets and fire.
- Commercial films: electronic pulse with synth pads.
- Two films (ANUTHAM, SUVARNABHOOMI) are carried by architect interviews
  cut in as black-and-white or desk-with-drawings talking heads.

## 2. Residential towers and townships

**ANUTHAM BY RAVI DESAI** (G4KLFCpR5GE, 3:09). Apartment towers, Ahmedabad;
9th Street Architects, Tierra Design landscape. Black-and-white architect
interview bookends the film; his lines drive the cut ("landscape and
architecture should have no boundaries", "the fifth elevation" over a
top-down courtyard). Interiors: bright daylight, polished grey concrete,
wood panelling, blue dining chairs, coffee-table close-up at shallow depth,
steaming pot in a white-marble kitchen. Night: symmetric two-tower hero.

**VENETIAN** (Bw-kFr7GcDE, 2:34). 3 and 4 BHK on the Sabarmati Riverfront;
HCP architects. Opens "Our Ambition Your Reality". Almost every shot is
static and in sunset light: riverfront with a huge sun behind the bridge,
louvers throwing stripes, a sheer curtain blowing in a bedroom, a red
espresso machine with croissants, a tiered gold pendant, a top-down through
a four-ring chandelier. Amenities include a marble prayer hall with three
crystal chandeliers and a golden arched portal, a pavilion with golden
jaali screens and pigeons, an outdoor cinema at night, skewers on a grill.

**THE VARANDA** (OS2HVHGaiHs, 2:16). Towers with a full amenity deck.
Opens on a macro palm frond, then a look-up through the canopy with a
starburst. Black Audi Q8 tracked into the porch; a couple walks through the
film. Lobby with floral mural, lap pool with trees on islands, rooftop
infinity pool, gymnastic rings on a tree branch with flare, glass badminton
court, banquet hall with a grid of glowing spheres. Music: "Auld Lang Syne"
with choir.

**WESTERN MARINA** (2_ojLiLywt4, 3:12). Hyderabad 4 BHK towers; Genesis
Planner. Opens on a real lake and hazy skyline with cranes, then a vibrant
drone push. Roughly thirty amenities, one frame each, all with 3D people:
spiral stair shot top-down, ball pit with giraffe mural, salon, pedicure
lounge, star-ceiling cinema, co-working, cafe. Finale: balconies at night
with people on them, then a city-grid night aerial. Electronic pulse score.

**APARNA AQUA** (Wygo0fgCQI4, 3:00). Hyderabad lakeside towers. Opens with a
flock of birds across a hazy sun, a top-down of teal water with white birds,
macro foliage with the lake in bokeh, a father and daughter on a path,
speedboats at a pier. Symmetrical tower reflection in the lake. Brand
moments: embroidered logo towel, lit club logo, "Tower 03" glass entrance.
Wellness beats: outdoor meditation, stretching on a green wall, yoga with a
garden window, ballet on a low stage. Finale: four searchlights into the sky,
fireworks over the lake.

**SUVARNABHOOMI, Rajkot** (3t72lxwhGvk, 2020, 3:23). Architect interviews
at a desk with drawings. Nature macro opener (dew, sprinklers, lotus,
flare). Explicitly Indian: brick entrance with a tiled roof, a curved
waterfall wall, wooden-pillar gazebos, clay urns, a jhoola close-up, a
golden Ganesha with lamp and flowers, pomegranates on marble, kitchen drawers
sliding open on their own.

**Serenity Lavish** (l6GUwVKACAM, 2018, 2:03) and **Swati Crimson**
(IgTHxA9y6Tk, 2018, 2:56): see sections 4 and 6.

**THE CASCADES** (6Jxizt5VsHA, Jan 2026, 1:36). Hyderabad 63-storey twin
towers with a skybridge. The studio's newest mode: a lifestyle film with a
recurring protagonist. Opens on sun shimmer on water, then a woman in
profile on a balcony at sunset. Yellow light-leak and bokeh transitions
between beats. Arrival is told in extreme close-ups: a winged hood
ornament, a grille blurring past, a hand pulling a flush door handle that
lights up on touch, then the car on the curved drive to the lit lobby. She
walks into a double-height lobby under a suspended-glass chandelier, then
appears in a lit white cabana at night (cool outside, warm inside), heels
on the deck in slow motion with her reflection in the pool, tracked from
behind through the landscape at sunset. Detail beats: sun through white
blossoms, a hand touching water with a single droplet sound, a
speed-ramped pour of sparkling wine, a dining table with gold cutlery and
white roses, a rack-focus reveal of the living room with two circular
chandeliers. Ethereal female vocals over synth pads, with foley for the
door handle, engine, footsteps, water and the pour. Ends on a dusk drone
of the towers over the lake and "A PROJECT BY" logos.

**CENTURY MIRAI** (tO-Vl1hLa6U, Bangalore, 3:34). The studio's
"day in the life" mode. Sunrise over forested hills, then a drone over
the complex; an alarm clock flips 05:59 to 06:00, a woman opens her eyes
(extreme close-up), taps the clock, sheer curtains slide open on their own;
top-down of the living room; three paper pendants; coffee pours from a
machine with steam; a couple at breakfast; she taps a wall screen showing a
delivery person with a face-recognition overlay and takes groceries at the
door; lobby and lift button "11"; small white flowers in wind; the "DELTA"
tower entrance; he taps a smartwatch and runs, an outdoor bike shows a
floating holographic workout readout; she stretches and holds a warrior
pose; a phone shows low battery and charges on a solar bench with a "30 %
wireless charger" graphic; laptop on the bench; pool with a waterfall, a
pink cocktail with mint, a white swan float; a straight-down drone of the
courtyard; a child on a skateboard and a scooter hug; the couple at dusk;
a street lamp switching on; a tennis ball toward camera; floodlit tennis
and pickleball from the air; basketball at night; fountains; "CLUB evolve"
sign, club lobby with a curved stair and an indoor tree; treadmill screen;
laptop, headphones and a clock at 9:03; business lounge with work pods;
VR zone; billiards top-down; foosball; games room with pink neon; tiered
green library with indoor trees; banquet table with white roses; champagne
pour; leather menu; sky bar; infinity pool with a "35 °C temperature
controlled pool" overlay; he takes a call in a tan suit, she in a gold
satin dress; car at a boom gate and round a fountain drive; friends arrive,
whiskey, laughter, an extreme close-up of her eyes; night aerials with
ground mist and a fly-through over the pool. Electronic synth-pop score,
dense foley (alarm, curtains, pour, footsteps, lift chime, racket, shaker,
ice). Product-feature overlays are drawn in as floating graphics.

## 3. Villas, bungalows, resorts

**LE PARQUE by GALA** (97jkvmDHgAk 2:47; Awn-m_M2M00 4:26; vwT-sdRWB-Y
2:45). 300-acre resort township, Bill Bensley. Three cuts of one film with
two scores (orchestral piano-and-strings, and an Indian fusion of flute,
sitar and percussion with a diegetic sound layer). Opens on a misty sunrise
aerial with god rays, a low track along mist on the water toward a lit
entrance portal, then a grey sedan followed from the air, from behind, and
laterally over a balustrade bridge. Landscape-first: organic farm plots from
the air, a glasshouse cafe, a rotunda clubhouse with a grand piano, a bronze
face fountain, a lagoon pool with rock edges and timber cabanas, a low
water-level shot to striped umbrellas, a villa door numbered 27 with a car
blurring past, kids playing football, a child followed through a white arch.
Night: rotunda reflected in a lily pond with a fire pit, entry road with
ground lights, an extreme aerial of the lit estate ringed by dark forest.
Credits on a light-grey textured card. Software credited on the studio site:
3ds Max, Forest Pack, Corona, Phoenix FD, GrowFX, Anima, DaVinci Resolve.

**Anaya** (n7kxiI1AB_k, 2:32). White townhouse community with a carved
white temple as the anchor. Acoustic guitar and piano. Product close-ups
every four or five shots (marble cup with succulents, teapot with purple
flowers, water lilies). The temple is first seen through wood slats. Logo on
treadmill screens.

## 4. Interior-led films

**Swati Crimson** (IgTHxA9y6Tk, 2018, 2:56). "4 BHK contemporary living";
ADS Architects, studio nilesh kava. The interiors come first: a static
close-up of a blue velvet ottoman with glasses on an open magazine, lilies in
a white vase, copper vase, Edison bulb cluster against dark wood panelling;
a slow pan across the master bedroom (low leather bed, striped rug, grey
textured stone headboard wall with one large circular abstract painting,
sheer beige curtains, bright midday); a living corner with two round dark
coffee tables, yellow flowers, tan leather armchair; a dining room with a
glass table, grey chairs, orchids, a floating white sideboard and a
sun-shaped mirror; a slow dolly into the kitchen with pancakes on the
breakfast bar and a bag of vegetables on the counter; a pan across the
open-plan room with a black chandelier and a wavy earth-toned rug; then the
balcony looking in through sliding glass, warm inside against a cool evening.
Only after that the exterior, the amenities (reading lounge with shelves
spelling READ, games room with carrom boards, banquet hall with a golden
tiered ceiling) and the pool at dusk with three waterfalls from a dark stone
wall under purple bougainvillea. Palette: earth tones, tan leather, grey
stone, one bold artwork per room, brass and copper accents.

**Serenity Lavish** (l6GUwVKACAM, 2018, 2:03). Nightstand close-up (clock,
yellow flowers, sculpture, textured headboard), bedroom with a sunset window,
one continuous slow pan from dining to lounge to the L-sofa and windows.
Night: terrace dining under Edison bulbs, a sunken fire pit with pillows and
lanterns.

Interior frames inside the tower films (ANUTHAM, VENETIAN, VARANDA, WESTERN
MARINA, SUVARNABHOOMI) follow the same recipe: one wide per room at 1.2 m,
one styled close-up, daylight through sheers, warm practicals at dusk, and
the window view always a real rendered garden or city.

## 5. Commercial films

**Swati CLOVER** (2WmNoZSkbZU, 1:30). Office building with ground-floor
retail. Particle-trail logo opener, electronic 4/4. Cool blue-grey glass
reflecting an overcast sky, then retail at golden hour with mannequins, a
tracking shot along storefronts, a corten-toned geometric block wall in
raking light, a top-down rotating lounge, a conference room, an open-plan
office with people, and a slow clockwise arc around a desk with a laptop and
a coffee cup.

**TROGON** (6HMF_LHDUyU, 1:32). Twin commercial towers; Mansi Shah
Architects. Extreme low-angle track up the glass, a silhouette of the towers
against a giant orange sun, sun glint with a flock of birds, a rooftop sky
garden from above, the plaza fountain seen in daylight and again at dusk as
the planter lights come on, a track across the night facade showing lit
office floors with people, a top-down from the tower roof into the lit
plaza, a night aerial with highway light streaks.

**WHERE LIVES THRIVE, Center Court by Trogon** (9Y6UqH41bYU, Aug 2025,
1:36). Residential, Mansi Shah Architects, Andy Fisher Workshop landscape.
The 2025 template: opens on tree reflections in still water with a jogger
crossing, a cooler greener daytime grade, many product-scale close-ups
(outdoor pillows, a billiard rack at shallow depth), a POV track down a row
of white-curtained cabanas, a symmetrical lap pool, an extreme low-angle at
three towers, then night with an outdoor mandir dressed in marigold garlands,
a circular fire pit, cabanas lit from within, and the building under a full
moon.

## 6. Evolution 2018 → 2026

| | 2018–2020 | 2023–2024 | 2025–2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runtime | 2:00–3:30 | 2:30–3:15 | 1:30–1:40 |
| Camera | static frames, slow pans | aerial glides, car tracking, top-down orbits | same moves, tighter cut |
| People | few or none | animated crowds everywhere | present but sparser, mid-distance |
| Palette | warm sunset throughout | vibrant saturated daylight plus dusk | cooler, greener daylight; night warmer |
| Sound | single piano track | guitar or Indian fusion plus diegetic sound | minimal ambient piano and pads |
| Opener | title card or towers at sunset | nature macro, birds, mist, god rays | reflection or macro with a person crossing |
| Finale | night aerial | searchlights, fireworks, city grid | full moon, fire pit, credits on light grey |
| Narrative | none; amenity inventory | couple or family walking through | a protagonist couple through one day with smart-living overlays (CENTURY MIRAI) or a lifestyle film with light leaks and slow motion (THE CASCADES) |
